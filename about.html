From freelancers to a full agency

Ello started in 2024 as a one-person operation building WordPress sites for local businesses. We quickly realized that most agencies were selling the same thing — templates with a logo slapped on top.

We decided to do things differently. Instead of churning out generic websites, we focused on building custom platforms that actually solve business problems. Real authentication systems. Working payment integrations. Admin dashboards that clients could actually use.

Today, we're a team of designers, developers, and strategists who've shipped over 50 projects across six industries. We still operate with the same philosophy: every line of code should serve a purpose.
